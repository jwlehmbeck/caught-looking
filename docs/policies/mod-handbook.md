# Mod handbook

Short version: keep the place kind, act fast, write it down, and leave the heavy cases to admins.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User | everyone | Report posts, comments, chat, polls and profiles. Block people. |
| Mod | trusted fans picked by an admin | Hide content, mute someone in chat for up to 24 hours, resolve or dismiss ordinary reports. |
| Admin | adults named by Jason | Everything mods can, plus suspend, ban, approve uploads, appoint and remove mods, handle urgent, under-13 and CSAM cases, generate invite codes. |

Every action by a mod or admin is logged in `mod_actions`. Nothing is secret from the other admins.

## What mods can and can't do

You can:

- **Hide** a post, comment or chat message. It disappears for everyone except staff and the author. Use this when something clearly breaks the house rules.
- **Mute** someone in chat for up to 24 hours when they keep it up after a hide.
- **Resolve** a report (you acted on it) or **dismiss** it (nothing wrong). Either way the whole group of reports on that item closes at once.

You can't:

- Ban or suspend anyone. Ask an admin.
- See or handle "may be under 13" or "urgent" reports. They never reach your queue. If you learn about one some other way, tell an admin right away.
- Act on reports about your own posts, comments, chat or profile. The console refuses. Ask another mod or an admin.
- Approve uploads. Admins do that.
- Contact a reported user privately about the report.

## Response targets

- **Urgent** (someone is in danger) and **under 13**: an admin takes a first look within **12 hours**. The app sends admins an alert the moment the report lands. If someone is in immediate danger, point them to 988 (call or text) or text HOME to 741741, and tell an admin.
- **Everything else**: first action within **48 hours**. The queue shows the most-reported items first; start at the top.

## Conflict of interest

If a report is about you, your friends, or someone you're arguing with, leave it for someone else. If you're unsure, leave it for someone else. Being fair matters more than being quick.

## How to decide

1. Read the snippet and the reporter notes. Open the item if you need context.
2. Ask: would a 13-year-old's parent be okay seeing this? Is it aimed at a person? Does it share contact info or try to move someone off the app?
3. Trash talk about teams is fine. Trash talk about a person is not. Anything that shares a phone number, handle, social app or link that's not on the allow-list goes.
4. Hide first, then resolve. Dismiss only when there's really nothing wrong.
5. Repeated problems from the same person: mute (mods) or ask an admin about a suspension.

## Sanctions a user can get

| Kind | Who can give it | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat mute | mod (up to 24 h) or admin | hours to days |
| Suspension | admin | days, usually 7 |
| Ban | admin | permanent |

The user sees the kind, the reason, the end date, and how to appeal.

## Appeals

A user who thinks a sanction or a hide was wrong can write to the admins at the contact address on the Contact page. Admins answer within 7 days. A mod never decides an appeal of their own action. An admin who issued the sanction asks another admin to review it when there is one.

## Things to never do

- Never screenshot and share reported content outside the console.
- Never ask a user for their age, real name, school or contact info.
- Never try to handle anything that looks like sexual content involving a minor. Hide it, stop, and message an admin with the post id only. The admin follows `csam-procedure.md`.
